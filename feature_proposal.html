Social Media Feature Proposals for Stock Prediction

General Construction Rules: * Horizons: standardized to \(H \in \{5, 21, 63, 252\}\) trading days (approx 1 week, 1 month, 1 quarter, 1 year). * No Look-ahead: All calculations use data strictly available at time \(t\). * Date Mapping: Leveraging Fama-French trading day counts where applicable.

1. Sentiment Dynamics (Momentum & Shifts)

Focusing on the change in opinion and the direction of that change.

  1. Sentiment Delta: Change in aggregate sentiment since \(t-H\). (\(S_t - S_{t-H}\)).
  2. Bear-to-Bull Flips (Count): Number of active users today who switched from Bearish (at most \(H\) days ago) to Bullish today.
  3. Bull-to-Bear Flips (Count): Number of active users today who switched from Bullish (at most \(H\) days ago) to Bearish today.
  4. Net Flip Count: (Bear-to-Bull Flips) - (Bull-to-Bear Flips).
  5. Bear-to-Bull Flip Ratio: (Bear-to-Bull Flips) / (Total Active Users).
  6. Bull-to-Bear Flip Ratio: (Bull-to-Bear Flips) / (Total Active Users).
  7. Conviction Index: % of users who have maintained the same sentiment direction for \(>K\) consecutive tweets (where \(K \in \{3, 5, 10\}\)).
  8. Reversal Magnitude: Net Sentiment\(_t\) minus MovingAverage(Net Sentiment, \(H\)).
  9. Extreme Bullish Consensus: Indicator if Bullish Ratio > 80% (or 90%).
  10. Extreme Bearish Consensus: Indicator if Bearish Ratio > 80% (or 90%).
  11. After-Hours Sentiment/Volume: Sentiment and Volume calculated specifically from Close (\(t\)) to Open (\(t+1\)).
  12. Market-Hours Sentiment/Volume: Sentiment and Volume calculated from Open (\(t\)) to Close (\(t\)).

2. User Cohort Composition (Who is talking?)

Segmenting the crowd by experience and activity gaps.

  1. Fresh Blood Count: Volume from users who have not tweeted about this stock in the last \(H\) days (where \(H \in \{21, 252, AllTime\}\)).
  2. Fresh Blood Ratio: (Fresh Blood Count) / (Total Volume).
  3. Re-entry Volume: Volume from users inactive on this stock for \(>H\) days but returned today.
  4. Whale Dominance: % of volume from top 1% users based on cumulative tweet count up to \(t-1\).
  5. Minnow Dominance: % of volume from users with \(<5\) tweets up to \(t-1\).
  6. Night-Owl Ratio: % of volume from users who post more than 80% of their tweets during After-Hours (rolling window classification).
  7. Day-Trader Ratio: % of volume from users who post more than 80% of their tweets during Market-Hours (rolling window classification).
  8. Retention Rate: % of users active today who posted at least one tweet about this stock in the previous \(H\) days.

3. Volume & Attention Dynamics

Measuring raw activity and silence.

  1. Volume Difference: Raw tweet count \(V_t\) minus \(V_{t-1}\).
  2. Log Volume Change: \(\ln(1+V_t) - \ln(1+V_{t-1})\) (Handles zero volume days).
  3. Abnormal Volume (Simple): \(V_t\) minus MovingAverage(\(V\), \(H=21\)).
  4. Silence Gap: Average time in days between consecutive tweets for stock \(j\) over the past \(H\) days (where \(H \in \{21, 252\}\)).
  5. Relative Volume: Stock Volume\(_t\) / Total Market Volume\(_t\).

4. Network & Interaction Structure

Focusing on sentiment divergence.

  1. Disagreement Index: \(1 - \frac{|N_{Bull} - N_{Bear}|}{N_{Bull} + N_{Bear}}\).

5. Intraday & Calendar Coverage

Full 24-hour cycle coverage ensuring no time period is missing.

  1. Pre-Market Activity: Volume/Sentiment from 04:00 to 09:30.
  2. Market-Open Frenzy: Volume/Sentiment from 09:30 to 10:00.
  3. Late Morning: Volume/Sentiment from 10:00 to 12:00.
  4. Mid-Day Lull: Volume/Sentiment from 12:00 to 13:00.
  5. Early Afternoon: Volume/Sentiment from 13:00 to 15:30.
  6. Closing Cross: Volume/Sentiment from 15:30 to 16:00.
  7. Post-Market Activity: Volume/Sentiment from 16:00 to 20:00.
  8. Overnight/Insomnia: Volume/Sentiment from 20:00 to 04:00 (Next Day).
  9. Intraday Volatility (Sessions): Standard deviation of sentiment across the 8 defined daily sessions.

6. Symbol-Specific & Contextual

  1. Symbol Focus: % of active users who tweeted only about this stock \(j\) today.
  2. Co-mention Count (Complexity): Average number of other symbols mentioned alongside stock \(j\) in today’s messages. (High = correlation/sector trade; Low = specific news).
  3. Broadcasting/Spam Ratio: % of messages about stock \(j\) that mention \(>5\) other stocks (Proxy for low-quality promotion).

(Note: Total count is 38 high-quality definitions. Expansions via Horizons (\(H\)) will create the desired volume of 50+ actual variables.)
